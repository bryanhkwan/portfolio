MAC TOURNAMENT VALIDATION

Monte Carlo + AI scouting report matched the tournament shape.

For Toledo's 2026 MAC Tournament run, I ran a simulation and opponent-scouting workflow that blended win-probability distributions with Gemini-assisted game-script analysis. The post-tournament validation showed strong score calibration and useful uncertainty framing.

Tournament readout

The model did not need to be perfect. It needed to be useful.

The strongest signal was not pretending the model could remove uncertainty. It correctly framed close-game risk, blowout paths, and late-game danger zones that actually mattered.

Bowling Green

Quarterfinal / Toledo 77, BG 76

Projected as a coin-flip finish and came in almost exactly there. The report flagged Toledo's jumper path, rim-finishing risk, Bowling Green three-point danger, and free throws as decisive.

UMass

Semifinal / Toledo by 10

The base projection was close, but the model assigned a real 30.4% Toledo blowout path. That scenario hit, with UMass jumper regression and game-script volatility showing up.

Akron

Championship / Akron by 3

The model projected Akron by 4.1 and the final margin was Akron by 3. It also flagged second-half explosiveness and late three-point danger, which became the final-game story.
